FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can join this platform?
The platform is open to all Karatina University students and members of the Nature Club. You can register as a Student to enroll in courses, or apply as an Instructor subject to administrator approval.
Are the courses free to access?
Yes! All courses on this platform are free for registered members of the Karatina University Nature Club. Simply register and start learning immediately.
How do I earn a certificate?
Complete all lessons in a course and meet the required completion criteria. The system will automatically generate a professional PDF certificate with a QR code for verification once you reach 100% progress.
How can I become an instructor?
Register for an account and select the "Instructor" role. Your application will be reviewed by the administrator. Once approved, you can create courses, upload materials, grade assignments, and host live classes.
How do live classes work?
Instructors add meeting links (Google Meet, Zoom, or Microsoft Teams) to their course. Students simply click "Join Class" at the scheduled time to join the session directly from their dashboard.
Can I download course materials?
Yes! Instructors upload PDF files, presentations, images, and other resources which you can download anytime from the course portal for offline study.
