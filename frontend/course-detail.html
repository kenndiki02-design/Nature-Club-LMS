Typography Fundamentals Completed

Lesson Notes

Typography is the art and technique of arranging type to make written language legible, readable, and appealing when displayed. The arrangement of type involves selecting typefaces, point sizes, line lengths, line-spacing (leading), and letter-spacing (tracking), and adjusting the space between pairs of letters (kerning). In this lesson, we will explore how typography influences user perception and how to combine fonts effectively for digital and print mediums.
