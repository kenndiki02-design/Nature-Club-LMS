Conserving Nature Through Education

At Karatina University Nature Club LMS, we believe that education is the foundation of effective conservation. Our platform bridges the gap between passionate environmentalists and the knowledge they need to make a real impact in the world.

Our goal is to foster a community of learners dedicated to sustainability, wildlife protection, and environmental stewardship.

  • Empowering individuals with practical skills
  • Fostering environmental awareness
  • Building a global community of eco-warriors

Our Journey

2020: Club Founded

KaruNature Club was established at Karatina University by a small group of passionate students.

2021: First Course Launched

We introduced our first local workshop on basic environmental conservation.

2022: 100 Students Reached

The club expanded its reach, educating over 100 students through seminars and field trips.

2023: LMS Launched

To digitize learning, the KaruNature LMS was built to offer accessible courses globally.

2024: 500+ Members

A thriving community with digital certifications, multiple instructors, and a growing course catalog.
